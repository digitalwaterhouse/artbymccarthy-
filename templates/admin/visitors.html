What is being counted

Two things count visitors on the site, and they do not agree — which is the point, not a fault.

  • Umami counts everyone. It sets no cookie, so it is not behind the Allow bar and it also records the people who said no to Google and the ones who never answered. It ignores crawlers, because a robot never runs the script.
  • Google Analytics counts only the visitors who pressed Allow. That is a smaller number, and not a random sample of the rest.

Neither is counted when you are signed in here — the studio carries no tag.
