What is being counted

Umami counts everyone who opens the site. It sets no cookie and stores nothing on the visitor's machine, so there is nothing to ask permission for — which is why there is no bar at the foot of the page any more.

  • It ignores crawlers, because a robot fetches the page and leaves without ever running the script. Counts taken from server logs do not make that distinction.
  • It counts people, not requests — a visitor reading six paintings is one visitor.

Neither is counted when you are signed in here — the studio carries no tag.
