{# --------------------------------------------------------------- plausible A visitor count that does not need to ask. WHY THIS SITS OUTSIDE THE CONSENT BAR while Google's tag sits inside it, and the distinction is the entire point of adding it: Plausible sets no cookie and stores nothing on the device. ePrivacy consent is about reading from or writing to a visitor's machine, so with nothing stored there is nothing to ask about -- which is why this script can run for everyone, including the people who said "No thanks" to Google and the people who never answered. That is the gap it fills. The consent bar means Google only ever counted the share of visitors who pressed Allow, and those are not a random sample, so the number was both smaller than the truth and bent in a direction nobody could measure. This counts the rest. The domain is a setting, pasted in the studio, exactly like the measurement ID -- nothing here names an account, and taking it off the site is emptying one box rather than a deploy. It must match the domain registered with Plausible or the script loads and reports into nowhere. NOT a replacement for Google's tag. Both can run; they answer different questions and disagreeing totals are expected, not a fault. #}
