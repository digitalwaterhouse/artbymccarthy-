{#- THE SWITCH. A real two-state toggle: sun at one end, moon at the other, and a knob that slides between them. role="switch" with aria-checked, which is the one ARIA pattern that means exactly this and nothing else. It STARTS wherever the device is -- somebody whose laptop turns dark at sunset finds it already dark -- and the first press pins a choice that then outlives the device's opinion. The knob's position is the whole label, so there is no wording to read; the screen-reader name says "Dark mode" and aria-checked says whether it is on. -#}
