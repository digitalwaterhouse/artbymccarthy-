{# ---- the box band ------------------------------------------------------ Between the hero and the wall, and still on the dark entry: the same reasoning that put the hero there. Every photograph on this site is straight-on, so the work reads flat, and the one fact that makes these objects rather than pictures -- that they are boxes, 2.5in deep, with things kept safe inside -- was nowhere a visitor could see it. Two halves, and each stands alone. The photograph is a box shot from an angle so the depth reads; until she takes one, the band renders as her sentence and the measurements, which is worth having on its own. The sentence is hers, verbatim, and is attributed so it reads as a painter talking about her work rather than as shop copy. #}
